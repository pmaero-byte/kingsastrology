## R-10.1 — Source of wealth by planet in the 10th house

- **Cites:** Ch 10, v 1
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** For each of the 10th house from Lagna and the 10th house from the Moon, identify any planet occupying that house. Map the occupying planet(s) to the source of wealth: Sun → father; Moon → mother; Mars → enemy; Mercury → friend; Jupiter → brother; Venus → wife; Saturn → servant. If the 10th from Lagna and the 10th from the Moon are both occupied, both sources apply; if several planets occupy a 10th house, there are several sources.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The planet sitting in your 10th house of work names the relationship through which your livelihood flows — father, mother, enemy, friend, brother, wife, or servant, per the fixed mapping. Where more than one planet occupies the 10th, or both the Lagna-10th and Moon-10th are tenanted, your wealth comes from several of these channels together. (Per Garga, noted by the translator, this manifests in the Dasa period of that planet.)
- **Verse (source):** "A person gets wealth from his father. mother, enemy. friend, brother, wife or servant according as the planet which occupies the 10th house from the Lagna or from the Moon tS the Sun, or the Moon, or Mar s or Mercury . or Iupiter or Venus or Saturn respectively (a). ## R-10.2 — Avocation by Navamsa lord of the 10th lord — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury

- **Cites:** Ch 10, v 2
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Let L10 = lord of the 10th house from Lagna, from the Moon, and from the Sun (three lords). For each, find the Navamsa sign it occupies, and take the lord of that Navamsa. When that Navamsa lord is:
  - Sun → livelihood in perfumes, gold, woollen fabric, medicine, and medical treatment of patients;
  - Moon → livelihood by tilling lands, dealing in productions of water (pearls, corals, conch-shells, whale-bones and the like), and through women (by serving under or following them);
  - Mars → livelihood by dealing in metals or minerals, fire (fire-engine driver, dealer in matches and the like), weapons, and by acts of boldness;
  - Mercury → livelihood as a writer, an accountant and the like (writing commentaries on the works of writers), and by various handicraft (painting, book-binding, arrow-making, flower-wreaths, sandal paste, perfumes).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The lord of the Navamsa occupied by your 10th lord names the craft and trade through which you earn. Sun gifts healing, medicine, perfumes, gold and woollen goods. Moon gifts agriculture, the yield of waters, and livelihood connected with women. Mars gifts metals, minerals, fire, weapons, and bold enterprise. Mercury gifts the pen — writing, accounts, commentary — and skilled handicraft. Each is a vocation gift to pursue with dignity.
- **Verse (source):** "Ir the lord of the Navamsa (0) be the Sun, a person will get wealth by dealing in perfumes, gold, woollen fabric, medicine and by medical treatment of patient s. If the lord be the Moon, he will get wealth by tilling lands, by dealing in the production s of water (b) and through women (e). If he be Mars, the person will get wealth by dealing in metals or minerals, fire (d) weapons and will also acquire wealth by acts of bold­ness; if he be Mercury, the person will be a writer , an accountant , and the like (e) and will also acquire wealth by various handicraft (f )."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR: "Ir" → "If"; "(0)" → "(a)"; "patient s" → "patients"; "production s" → "productions". Translator footnotes: (a) "the lord of the Navamsa occupied by the lord of the 10th house from Lagna, the Moon and the Sun"; (b) productions of water = pearls, corals, conch-shells, whale-bones; (c) through women = by serving under or otherwise following them; (d) fire = fire-engine driver, dealer in matches and the like; (e) writer/accountant = by writing commentaries on the works of writers; (f) handicraft = painting, book-binding, arrow-making, flower-wreaths, sandal paste, perfumes. Operates in the Antardasa periods of such lords (see v 3 note h).

## R-10.3 — Avocation by Navamsa lord of the 10th lord — Jupiter, Venus, Saturn

- **Cites:** Ch 10, v 3
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Continuing from R-4.2, when the Navamsa lord of a 10th lord is:
  - Jupiter → livelihood through Brahmins, the Devas or temple men, through mines (akuḍa — gold/silver mines, salt-producing places, elephant-forests) and the like (contract work), and through acts of virtue (sacrificial rites, gifts, charity, fasting, pilgrimage, service under Guru);
  - Venus → livelihood by dealing in gems (diamonds, rubies and the like), silver and other metals, in cows and buffaloes;
  - Saturn → livelihood by means of hard labour (walking and the like), by acts of torture (self-torture or torturing other creatures), by carrying burdens and by low deeds unsuited to his rank.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Jupiter gifts livelihood through the holy, the temple, mines and contracts, and through acts of virtue — a vocation of service to sacred order. Venus gifts livelihood in gems, silver and metals, and in cattle — a vocation of beauty and substance. Saturn indicates livelihood by hard labour, carrying burdens, and work of humble rank; this is a tend indication offset by the truth that honest labour and bearing burdens are themselves dignified, and the other avocation gifts in the chart (R-4.2 / R-4.3 Jupiter, Venus, or the R-4.4 friendly-sign and benefic placements) name the channels through which effort ripens into steady gain.

## R-10.4 — Source of wealth by sign-dignity of the yoga planet, and benefics in 11th/Lagna/2nd

- **Cites:** Ch 10, v 4
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Let a "yoga planet" be either (a) a planet occupying the 10th house from the Lagna or from the Moon, or (b) the lord of the Navamsa occupied by the lord of the 10th house from the Lagna, the Moon, or the Sun. At birth, test the sign dignity of each yoga planet:
  - yoga planet in a friendly sign → wealth (in its Antardasa) through a friend;
  - yoga planet in an inimical sign → wealth through an enemy;
  - yoga planet in its own house → wealth by means of, or from, his own house;
  - when the Sun is the yoga planet and occupies its exaltation sign (Aries) → wealth by his own powers.
  Separately, if powerful benefic planets occupy the 11th house, the Lagna, or the 2nd house, the person acquires wealth in various ways (prospers well whatever avocation he may choose to follow).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The dignity of your yoga planet refines the channel of wealth: a friendly sign brings it through friends; an inimical sign, through an adversary; the planet's own house brings it from one's own home and means; and the Sun exalted in Aries grants wealth by your own powers — self-made mastery. Where powerful benefics stand in the 11th, the Lagna, or the 2nd, prosperity runs wide: whatever honest avocation you choose to follow, it flourishes.
