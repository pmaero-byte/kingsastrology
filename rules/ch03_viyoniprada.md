> This chapter treats **non-human and non-uterine births** (lower animals, birds, trees) — the edge cases of horoscopy. For the ordinary human natal reading it is background doctrine that supports horary/lost-property questions and the Dwadasamsa/Navamsa machinery used elsewhere. Rules here are mostly `table`/`neutral`. No rule in this chapter is used to make a statement about a *person's* worth or nature.

## R-3.1 — Non-uterine birth by Dwadasamsa of the Moon

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 1
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** at birth (or query), malefics powerful, benefics weak, and a hermaphrodite planet (Mercury or Saturn) in a Kendra or aspecting the Lagna
- **Effect:** The creature born is that indicated by the particular Dwadasamsa the Moon then occupies. The number of offspring equals the number of Dwadasamsas passed over by the Moon; odd Dwadasamsas = male issue, even = female; the number that perish immediately equals the Dwadasamsas occupied by malefics or otherwise weak.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, the malefic planets be powerful, the benefic planets weak, and one of the hermaphrodite planets either be in one of the Kendras (angles) or aspect the Lagna (ascendant); the creature born will be that indicated by the particular Dwadasamsa which the Moon might then occupy."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Example in source: Moon at 21° Leo → 9th Dwadasamsa of Leo = Pisces → a fish. Hermaphrodite planets per Ch II v 6 (Mercury, Saturn). Kendras per Ch I v 17. Applies at birth or at time of query (commentator).

## R-3.2 — Lower-animal birth when Lagna is non-human

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 2
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** malefics in their own Navamsas and powerful, benefics not in own Navamsas and weak, and the Lagna (rising sign) other than human
- **Effect:** The birth is that of a lower animal, indicated by the Dwadasamsa the Moon then occupies. (Human signs = Gemini, Virgo, Libra, second half of Sagittarius, Aquarius.)
- **Verse (source):** "If the malefic planets occupy their Navamsas and be powerful, if the benefic planets be not in their own Navamsas and be weak and if (at the same time) the Lagna (rising sign) be other than human, the birth will be that of a lower animal as before."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Human signs defined in source note (a).

## R-3.3 — Body parts of a quadruped mapped to the 12 signs

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 3
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (body-part lookup for quadrupeds; for birds, wings = forelegs)
- **Effect:** For quadrupeds: Aries=head, Taurus=face & neck, Gemini=forelegs & shoulders, Cancer=back, Leo=breast, Virgo=sides, Libra=belly, Scorpio=anus, Sagittarius=hind legs, Capricorn=penis/testicles, Aquarius=buttocks, Pisces=tail. (For birds, wings stand for forelegs.) This also ascertains the color/wounds of particular body parts.
- **Verse (source):** "In the case of quadrupeds, Aries is the head; Taurus, the face and neck; Gemini, the forelegs and shoulders; Cancer, the back; Leo, the breast; Virgo, the sides; Libra, the belly; Scorpio, the anus; Sagittari, the hind legs; Capricorn, the penis or testicle; Aquarius, the buttocks and Pisces, the tail."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Horary body-part table; the Kalapurusha mapping (R-1.4) is the human analogue.

## R-3.4 — Color of the creature

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 4
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** planet(s) in the rising sign → color of that planet; else planet aspecting rising sign; else color of the rising Navamsa
- **Effect:** If any planet occupies the rising sign, the creature's color is that planet's color; if not, the color of the planet aspecting the rising sign; if none aspect, the color of the rising Navamsa. The number/variety of colors = the planets occupying or aspecting the rising sign (the powerful planet's color predominates). Stripes on the back = planets in the 7th house.
- **Verse (source):** "If there be any planet in the rising sign, the color of the creature will be that of the planet itself; if not, the color will be that of the planet aspecting the rising sign; and if no planets aspect the rising sign the color will be that of the rising Navamsa. The number and variety of colors will be that of the planets occupying or aspecting the rising sign. The number of stripes on the back of the creature will be that of the planets occupying the seventh house."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Planet colors per Ch II v 4–5.

## R-3.5 — Land-bird vs water-bird

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 5
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** a Pakshi-drekkana, or a Chara-navamsa, or a Navamsa of Mercury rises, occupied by powerful planets; and is occupied or aspected by Saturn (→ land bird) or by the Moon (→ water bird)
- **Effect:** Under these rising conditions with powerful planets, the creature is a land bird if the rising Drekkana/Navamsa is occupied or aspected by Saturn, and a water bird if occupied or aspected by the Moon.
- **Verse (source):** "If a Pakshidrekkana or a Charanavamsa or a Navamsa of Mercury should rise and be occupied by powerful planets, the creature born would be a land bird if the rising Drekkana or Navamsa be either occupied or aspected by Saturn; and a water bird if it be occupied or aspected by the Moon."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Pakshi-drekkanas = 2nd Drekkana of Gemini, 1st of Leo, 2nd of Libra, 1st of Aquarius. Chara-navamsas = the movable Navamsas. Mercury-navamsas = those of Gemini & Virgo.

## R-3.6 — Vegetable birth (tree) and its count

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 6
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** the rising sign, the Moon, Jupiter, and the Sun are all weak
- **Effect:** These indicate the birth of a tree; a land tree or water tree according as the rising Navamsa is one of land or water. The number of trees = the number of signs by which the lord of the ascendant Navamsa has receded from the rising sign; trebled if that lord is in exaltation or retrograde; doubled if in Vargottama, own Navamsa, own sign, or own Drekkana.
- **Verse (source):** "If the rising sign, the Moon, Jupiter and the Sun should be weak, these indicate the birth of a tree; such tree will be either a land tree or a water tree according as the rising Navamsa is one of land or of water. The number of trees will be the number of Signs by which the lord of the ascendant Navamsa has receded from the rising sign."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Water Navamsas = those of Cancer, 2nd half of Makara's Navamsa, Pisces; others are land. Multipliers per source note (b).

## R-3.7 — Tree character by the ascendant-Navamsa lord

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 7
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** lord of the ascendant Navamsa is a given planet (other conditions of v 6 holding)
- **Effect:** If the ascendant-Navamsa lord is the Sun → a tree strong within; Saturn → an ugly tree; the Moon → a milky tree; Mars → a thorny tree; Jupiter → a fruit tree; Mercury → a fruitless tree; Venus → a flower tree; the Moon (again) → an oily tree; Mars (again) → a sour-taste tree.
- **Verse (source):** "If the lord of the ascendant Navamsa be the Sun, the tree will be one strong within; if Saturn, the tree will be an ugly one; if the Moon, it will be a milky tree; if Mars, a thorny tree; if Jupiter, a fruit tree; if Mercury, a fruitless tree; if Venus, a flower tree; if the Moon again, an oily tree; and if Mars, a tree of sour taste."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Conditions of stanza 6 remain the same (source note).

## R-3.8 — Superior/inferior tree by benefic–malefic cross

- **Cites:** Ch 3, v 8
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** lord of the ascendant Navamsa is a benefic in a malefic sign (→ superior tree on bad ground); or a malefic in a benefic house (→ inferior tree on good ground)
- **Effect:** If the ascendant-Navamsa lord is a benefic in a malefic sign, the tree is a superior one growing on bad ground; the reverse (malefic lord in a benefic house) gives an inferior tree on good ground. The number of trees = the number of Navamsas by which that lord has receded from its Navamsa.
- **Verse (source):** "If the lord of the ascendant Navamsa be a benefic planet occupying a malefic sign, the tree will be a superior one growing on a bad ground; if otherwise, the reverse will be the case; the number of trees is also the number of Navamsas by which the lord of the ascendant Navamsa has receded from his Navamsa."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Benefic/malefic per Ch II v 5. This stanza closes Chapter III.
