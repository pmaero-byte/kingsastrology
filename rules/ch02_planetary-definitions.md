## R-2.1 — Planetary significations & royal stations  *(critical)*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 1
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (signification lookup)
- **Effect:** To the Kalapurusha: the Sun = soul, the Moon = mind, Mars = strength, Mercury = speech, Jupiter = knowledge & health, Venus = desire, Saturn = sorrow. Among the planets, the Sun & Moon are kings, Mars is the general, Mercury is the first prince, Jupiter & Venus are counsellors, and Saturn is the servant.
- **Verse (source):** "To the Kalapurusha, the Sun is the soul, the Moon is the mind, Mars is strength, Mercury is speech, Jupiter is knowledge and health, Venus is desire, and Saturn is sorrow. Of the planets, the Sun and the Moon are kings, Mars is general, Mercury is the first prince, Jupiter and Venus are counsellors and Saturn is servant."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical — the meaning of each planet used throughout. Saturn = sorrow is rendered in readings as "that which asks for patience/endurance," paired with its stewardship gift (see R-2.7, R-2.21).

## R-2.4 — Colors of the planets

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 4
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (planet-color lookup)
- **Effect:** The Sun is red & dark-brown; the Moon is white; Mars is not tall and is red-and-white; Mercury is green like bent grass; Jupiter is yellow like molten gold; Venus is neither very white nor very black; Saturn is black. A person's color follows the most powerful planet at birth.
- **Verse (source):** "The Sun is of a red and dark-brown color; the Moon is white; Mars is not of a tall figure and is both red and white; Mercury is green like the color of the bent grass; Jupiter is yellow like molten gold; Venus is neither very white nor very black, and Saturn is black."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Color of the powerful planet at birth gives the native's complexion (source note).

## R-2.5 — Presidencies, Devatas, directions, malefic/benefic  *(critical]*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 5
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (lookup)
- **Effect:** Color presidency: Sun=copper, Moon=white, Mars=red, Mercury=green, Jupiter=yellow, Venus=mixed, Saturn=black. Devatas: Sun=Agni, Moon=Varuna, Mars=Subrahmanya, Mercury=Vishnu, Jupiter=Indra, Venus=Indrani, Saturn=Brahma. Directions: Sun=east, Venus=south-east, Mars=south, Rahu=south-west, Saturn=west, Moon=north-west, Mercury=north, Jupiter=north-east. Malefics: the waning Moon, the Sun, Mars, Saturn, and Mercury when conjunct any of these. (Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury when conjunct either of these benefic groups are benefics.)
- **Verse (source):** "The Sun presides over copper color; the Moon over white color; Mars over red color; Mercury over green color; Jupiter over yellow color; Venus over a mixture of various colors; and Saturn over black color. The Sun is Agni; the Moon is Varuna; Mars is Subrahmanya; Mercury is Vishnu; Jupiter is Indra; Venus is Indrani (Indra's wife) and Saturn is Brahma. The Sun presides over east; Venus, south-east; Mars, south; Rahu, south-west; Saturn, west; the Moon, north-west; Mercury, north; Jupiter, north-east. The waning Moon, the Sun, Mars, Saturn and Mercury when in conjunction with any of these, are malefic planets."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Benefic/benefic classification is the functional core. Jupiter, Venus, and waxing Moon are natural benefics; Mars, Saturn, Sun, waning Moon natural malefics; Mercury mutable (benefic with benefics, malefic with malefics).

## R-2.6 — Genders & elements

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 6
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (lookup)
- **Effect:** Hermaphrodite planets: Mercury & Saturn. Female planets: Moon & Venus. Male planets: the rest (Sun, Mars, Jupiter). Elements: Mars=fire, Mercury=earth, Jupiter=akasa (ether), Venus=water, Saturn=air (and the Sun=fire, the Moon=water).
- **Verse (source):** "Mercury and Saturn are hermaphrodite planets, the Moon and Venus are female planets, and the rest are male planets. Mars is fire; Mercury is earth; Jupiter is akas (ether); Venus is water and Saturn is air."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Source note: Mercury is female-hermaphrodite, Saturn male-hermaphrodite.

## R-2.7 — Castes & gunas

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 7
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (lookup)
- **Effect:** Castes: Venus & Jupiter = Brahmin; Mars & the Sun = Kshatriya; the Moon = Vaisya; Mercury = Sudra; Saturn = Chandala. Gunas: the Moon, the Sun, and Jupiter are of Satwa-guna (good temper); Mercury and Venus are of Rajo-guna (passionate temper); Mars and Saturn are of Tamo-guna (dark temper).
- **Verse (source):** "Venus and Jupiter are Brahmins; Mars and the Sun are Kshatriyas; the Moon is a Vaisya; Mercury is a Sudra and Saturn is a Chandala. The Moon, the Sun and Jupiter are of Satwaguna (good temper); Mercury and Venus are of Rajoguna (passionate temper); Mars and Saturn are of Tamoguna (dark temper)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Caste terms are classical classifiers of planetary temperament, not social verdicts on persons. In readings they map to temperament/role (scholarly, warrior/leadership, mercantile, skilled-service, austere-outcaste-currents) — rendered as tendencies, never as a person's social worth.

## R-2.11 — Body-components, presidences, garments, metals & seasons

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 11 (with v 12)
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (lookup)
- **Effect:** Muscular strength: Saturn in muscles, Sun in bones, Moon in blood, Mercury in skin, Venus in semen, Jupiter in flesh, Mars in marrow. Presidences: Sun=place of worship, Moon=wells, Mars=fire-place, Mercury=playgrounds, Jupiter=store-room, Venus=bed-room, Saturn=places where sweepings are gathered. Garments: Sun=thick thread, Moon=new, Mars=partly burnt, Mercury=wet, Jupiter=somewhat used, Venus=strong, Saturn=torn. Metals: Sun=copper, Moon=gems, Mars=gold, Mercury=brass, Jupiter=silver (gold when in his own house), Venus=pearls, Saturn=iron. Season by planet in ascendant or its Drekkana rising: Saturn=Sisira (Jan–Feb), Venus=Vasanta (Mar–Apr), Sun/Mars=Greeshma (May–Jun), Moon=Varsha (Jul–Aug), Mercury=Sarat (Sep–Oct), Jupiter=Hemanta (Nov–Dec).
- **Verse (source):** "Saturn is strong in muscles, the Sun in bones, the Moon in blood, Mercury in skin, Venus in semen, Jupiter in flesh, and Mars in the marrow of the bones. … The garment of the Sun is of thick thread; that of the Moon is new; that of Mars is partly burnt; that of Mercury is wet; that of Jupiter is somewhat used; that of Venus is strong; and that of Saturn is torn. The Sun presides over copper; the Moon over gems; Mars over gold; Mercury over brass; Jupiter over silver; Venus over pearls; and Saturn over iron. When either Saturn is in the ascendant or when his Drekkana is rising, the season denoted is Sisira …" (seasons continue per planet as above)
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Consolidates stanzas 11–12 descriptive tables. Used in horary/colour/season readings.

## R-2.13 — Planetary aspects (Drishti)  *(critical]*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 13
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (aspect computation)
- **Effect:** All planets aspect the 3rd & 10th houses with a quarter sight; the 5th & 9th with half a sight; the 4th & 8th with three-quarters; the 7th with a full sight. **Special:** Saturn aspects the 3rd & 10th with a *full* sight; Jupiter aspects the 5th & 9th with a *full* sight; Mars aspects the 4th & 8th with a *full* sight. (Aspects extend 15° on either side of the house-middle points.)
- **Verse (source):** "All the planets aspect the 3rd and 10th houses with a quarter sight; the 5th and 9th houses with half a sight, the 4th and 8th houses with three-quarters of a sight, and the 7th house with a full sight; but Saturn aspects the 3rd and 10th houses with a full sight; Jupiter aspects the 5th and 9th houses with a full sight, and Mars aspects the 4th and 8th houses with a full sight."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical — the aspect computation used by Chapter XIX (aspects) and every yoga/aspection rule. The aspect-strength fractions (¼, ½, ¾, full) are part of the table.

## R-2.14 — Planetary durations & flavors presidencies

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 14
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (lookup)
- **Effect:** Durations signified: the Sun=six months; the Moon=two ghatikas (48 minutes); Mars=a day; Mercury=two months; Jupiter=a month; Venus=a fortnight; Saturn=a year. Flavors: Sun=pungent, Moon=salt, Mars=acerbity, Mercury=mixed, Jupiter=sweetness, Venus=sourness, Saturn=bitterness.
- **Verse (source):** "The Sun indicates six months; the Moon two Ghatikas or forty-eight minutes; Mars a day; Mercury two months; Jupiter a month; Venus a fortnight; and Saturn a year. The Sun presides over pungent flavor; the Moon over salt flavor; Mars over acerbity; Mercury over mixed flavor; Jupiter over sweetness; Venus over sourness; and Saturn over bitterness."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Durations feed period/timing reasoning; flavors feed horary.

## R-2.16 — Permanent friendships (the Author's canon)  *(critical]*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 16–17
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (permanent-relation lookup)
- **Effect:** Permanent friends/enemies/neutrals (the Author's own table, following Satyacharya): 
  - **Sun:** friends = Moon, Mars, Jupiter; enemy = Saturn, Venus; neutral = Mercury.
  - **Moon:** friends = Sun, Mercury; neutrals = the rest; (the Moon has no enemies).
  - **Mars:** friends = Sun, Moon, Jupiter; enemy = Mercury; neutrals = Venus, Saturn.
  - **Mercury:** friends = Sun, Venus; enemy = Moon; neutrals = the rest (Mars, Jupiter, Saturn).
  - **Jupiter:** friends = Sun, Moon, Mars; enemies = Mercury, Venus; neutral = Saturn.
  - **Venus:** friends = Mercury, Saturn; neutrals = Mars, Jupiter; enemies = the rest (Sun, Moon).
  - **Saturn:** friends = Venus, Mercury; neutral = Jupiter; enemies = the rest (Sun, Moon, Mars).
- **Verse (source):** "Saturn and Venus are the enemies of the Sun, Mercury is his neutral, and the rest are his friends. The Sun and Mercury are the friends of the Moon and the rest are her neutrals. The Sun, the Moon, and Jupiter are the friends of Mars; Mercury is his enemy, and Venus and Saturn are his neutrals. The Sun and Venus are the friends of Mercury; the Moon is his enemy, and the rest are his neutrals. Mercury and Venus are the enemies of Jupiter; Saturn is his neutral, and the rest are his friends. Mercury and Saturn are the friends of Venus; Mars and Jupiter are her neutrals, and the rest are her enemies. Venus and Mercury are the friends of Saturn; Jupiter is his neutral, and the rest are his enemies."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical relation table. Derived by Satyacharya's rule: a planet's friends are the lords of the 2nd, 12th, 5th, 9th, 8th, and 4th from its Moolatrikona sign plus the lord of its exaltation sign (excluding conflicts); enemies are the lords of the remaining houses (excluding conflicts); those in both are neutral. Stanza 15 gives the same by "some" (a simpler list); the Author's canon (16–17) is used here.

## R-2.18 — Temporary relations & the five combined relations

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 18
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** planets occupying the 2nd, 12th, 11th, 3rd, 10th, or 4th from a given planet are its temporary friends; the rest are temporary enemies. (Some add planets in a planet's exaltation sign as temporary friends.)
- **Effect:** Combine the permanent relation (R-2.16) with the temporary relation to get the five relations: Adimitra (great friend) = permanent friend + temporary friend; Atisatru (great enemy) = permanent enemy + temporary enemy; Mitra (friend) = neutral + temporary friend; Satru (enemy) = neutral + temporary enemy; Sama (neutral) = permanent friend + temporary enemy (or permanent enemy + temporary friend).
- **Verse (source):** "Planets that occupy the 2nd, 12th, 11th, 3rd, 10th and 4th houses from a particular planet are the friends of that planet and the rest are his enemies for the time. According to some, the planets that occupy the exaltation sign of a particular planet are also the friends of that planet. Combining together the three natural relations … with the two relations for the time now given, we may determine the five following relations existing between planets: Adimitra (great friend), Mitra (friend), Sama (neutral), Satru (enemy) and Atisatru (great enemy)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical — the five-relation system feeds dignity/strength and yoga judgment.

## R-2.19 — Sthana bala (positional) & Dik bala (directional)  *(critical]*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 19
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** positional strength when a planet is in its exaltation sign, a friendly sign, its Moolatrikona, its own Navamsa, or its own Kshetra (house). Dik bala: Mercury & Jupiter powerful in the East (rising sign/lagna); the Sun & Mars powerful in the South (10th sign); Saturn powerful in the West (setting/7th sign); the Moon & Venus powerful in the North (4th sign, Nadir).
- **Effect:** A planet has Sthana-bala (positional strength) when in exaltation, friendly sign, Moolatrikona, own Navamsa, or own sign. Dik-bala (directional strength): Mercury & Jupiter are strong in the East (ascendant); Sun & Mars in the South (10th); Saturn in the West (7th); Moon & Venus in the North (4th).
- **Verse (source):** "A planet has sthanabala (local or positional strength) when in his exaltation sign, friendly sign, Mulatrikona, in his Navamsa, or in his Kshetra house. Mercury and Jupiter are powerful when in the East or rising sign. The Sun and Mars are powerful when on the South or tenth sign. Saturn is powerful when in the West or setting sign, and the Moon and Venus are powerful when in the North or the 4th sign (Patala)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical strength table. Some commentators extend Dik bala to the triangular sign-sets of each direction (source note).

## R-2.20 — Cheshta bala (motional strength)

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 20
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** motional strength — Sun & Moon in any of the six signs from Capricorn; the other planets in retrograde motion, or in conjunction with the Moon (Samagama), or at greatest brilliancy, or when the northern planet in a planetary conjunction (Yuddha).
- **Effect:** The Sun and Moon possess Cheshta-bala (motional strength) in any of the six signs from Capricorn. The other planets possess it when retrograde, when conjoined with the Moon, at their greatest brilliancy (Mercury ~29° from Sun, Venus ~41° from Sun, others in opposition to Sun), or when they are the northern planet in a planetary war (Yuddha).
- **Verse (source):** "The Sun and the Moon possess Cheshtabala (motional strength) when in any of the six signs from Makara. The other planets possess it when in their retrograde motion or when in conjunction with the Moon (Samagama) or when in their greatest brilliancy or when they happen to be the northern planets in planetary conjunction (Yuddha)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Strength table; "greatest brilliancy" thresholds per source note.

## R-2.21 — Kala bala (temporal strength) & Naisargika bala (natural strength)  *(critical]*

- **Cites:** Ch 2, v 21
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** temporal strength — Moon, Mars, Saturn strong at night; Mercury both day & night; the others (Sun, Jupiter, Venus) by day. Malefics strong during the waning Moon (Krishna paksha); benefics during the waxing Moon (Sukla paksha). Each planet has Kala-bala in its own year, month, day, and hour. Natural strength order (Naisargika bala): Saturn > Mars > Mercury > Jupiter > Venus > Moon > Sun — each naturally stronger than the immediately preceding in that order.
- **Effect:** Kala-bala (temporal strength): the Moon, Mars and Saturn are strong at night; Mercury by both day and night; the Sun, Jupiter and Venus by day. Malefics gain strength during the waning Moon; benefics during the waxing Moon; and each planet is strong in its own year, month, day and hour. Naisargika-bala (natural strength): Saturn is naturally strongest, then Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun — each stronger than the one before it in that order.
- **Verse (source):** "The Moon, Mars and Saturn possess Kalabala (temporal strength) at night; Mercury possesses it both day and night; and the other planets possess it by day. The malefic planets possess it during the waning Moon (Krishna paksha) and the benefic planets during the waxing Moon (Sukla paksha). Also each planet has it in his year, month, day and hour. Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, the Moon and the Sun are each naturally stronger than the immediately preceding planet in the order stated."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical. The source closes with the operational reminder: before applying the astrological truths in later chapters, consider the strength/weakness of each house, its lord, and the planets occupying or aspecting it — using actual longitudes (sphuta) from Revati, not a rough chart. This is the charter's "round-Earth, true longitudes" principle encoded in the source itself.
