# Chapter 4 — Graha Rasis (Sun, Mars and other planets in the several signs of the Zodiac)

- **Source:** Brihat Jataka, Chapter 4 (translator's page header "CH. XVIII")
- **Source text:** sourcetext/pdfcoffee.com_brihat-jataka-of-varahamihira-pdf-free.pdf
- **Runtime use:** deterministic, one-way; no AI at runtime
- **Authoring:** drafted (AI-assisted) → human scholar review → frozen
- **Status legend:** see §5 of rules/README.md

> Note on scope: This chapter describes the six grahas Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn in the twelve signs. The Moon's placement-effects are treated in the preceding chapter (Ch 4, Moon in signs) and are explicitly cross-referenced by verse 13 (Jupiter in Aquarius) and verse 20 of this chapter. No Moon-in-sign rules are authored here; see `rules/ch18_moon-in-signs.md`. Verse 20 and the appended "Satyacharya's work on Horoscopy" rising-sign descriptions (lagna effects) are bhava/lagna doctrine, not planet-in-sign placements, and are recorded under Notes only — not as R-19 rules.

> Verse numbering follows the translator's "1.", "2.", … markers in the source. Where a single verse describes two signs together (e.g. "Mars in sign Aries or Scorpio"), one rule is written per sign, each citing the same verse and sharing the effect text, with the Condition scoped to that single sign.

## R-18.Sun.Aries — Sun in Aries

- **Cites:** Ch 18, v 1 (and Note a)
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Sun in sign Aries
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Wide-spread fame and capable, with a restless, far-roving temperament; wealth stays modest and the person keeps tools of protection close. In the exaltation degree specifically, the gift ripens — great riches, a settled abode, attendants bearing arms, and great fame and ability.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Sun in Aries but not in its exaltation degree (a) will be of wide-spread fame and ability of wandering habits, possessed of small wealth and will carry weapons." Note (a): "A person born with the Sun in the exaltation degree will be very rich, will remain for ever in a place, will have servants carrying arms, will be of great fame and ability."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR-corrected ":md" → "and", "habil s" → "habits". Translator Note (a) gives the exaltation-degree variant; treat exaltation-degree sub-case as a refinement for scholar review.

- **Verse 20 (bhava/lagna doctrine, not a planet-in-sign rule):** "Effects similar to those described for the several signs of the Zodiac when the Moon occupies them and when she is aspected by planets are mentioned also for the rising sign; nay more effects are due to the rising sign and to the 2nd, 3rd and other bhavas; if the houses be powerful and if their lords be also powerful, the objects signified by them will be promoted, and reduced, if weak." Translator Note: "If the rising sign and its lord be powerful, a person born will be of strong physique; if the 2nd house and its lord be powerful, the person will be rich. Similar remarks apply to the other bhavas." This is bhava-weighting doctrine and belongs to the bhava chapter (Ch 21) — recorded here for cross-reference only, not as an R-19 rule.
