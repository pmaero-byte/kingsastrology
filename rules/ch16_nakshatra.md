## R-16.0 — Moon-power modifier (chapter-level)

- **Cites:** Ch 16, closing note (after v 14)
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Moon is powerful (dignity strong by the Chapter 4 dignity rules — waxing Moon, or in own/exalted/friendly/kendra houses as the dignity engine determines)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The temperament effects given for the Moon's nakshatra (R-4.1..R-4.27) come to pass fully. When the Moon is weak, the same traits appear only in part and are to be tended rather than asserted as gifts. Honour the Moon — keep the mind clean, the body fed and rested, the emotions steady — so the nakshatra's gifts can ripen.
- **Verse (source):** "The effects described above will fully come to pass only if the Moon be powerful."

## R-16.6 — Ardra

- **Cites:** Ch 16, v 3
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** Moon in nakshatra Ardra
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Insincere, of irascible temper, ungrateful, indulging in torture, addicted to wicked deeds. These are hard tendencies to tend, not a fate — Ardra is the storm-star; its constructive gift is the fierce energy itself, which when disciplined becomes the courage to face what others avoid. The work is to convert the irascibility into truth-telling and the insincerity into strategy, gratitude into a daily practice. (Full when the Moon is powerful — see R-4.0.)
- **Verse (source):** "A person born when the Moon passes through the asterism of Ardra will be Insincere; of irascible temper, ungrateful. indulge in torture and be addicted to wicked deeds."
