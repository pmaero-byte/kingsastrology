## R-17.1 — Moon in Aries

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 1
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Aries (Mesha)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A quick, restless, passionate temperament — fond of travel, fond of women, fond of hot food taken in moderation and of vegetable food. You relent quickly: anger flares and passes. The same fire gives boldness in fight and a capacity to serve well under others. Wealth comes and goes rather than settling; the body bears the sign of motion — weak knees, disfigured nails, a wounded head, round red eyes. You are the eldest of your brothers, haughty, fickle-minded, afraid of water, with palm-lines shaped like the Sakti weapon. The fleetingness is the price of an enterprising, energetic nature; channel the mobility toward purposeful ventures rather than scattering it.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Aries will have round red eyes, will be fond of vegetable food and will eat hot meals but in a moderate degree; will be of a quickly relenting nature, will be fond of travels and of sexual union; will be of weak knees; will possess no permanent wealth; will be fond of fight and of women; will be skilled in serving under other men; will have disfigured nails and wounded head; will be haughty, will be the eldest of his brothers; will have lines in his hand of the shape of the weapon known as Sakti; will be fickle-minded and will be afraid of water."

## R-17.2 — Moon in Taurus

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 2
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Taurus (Vrishabha)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A fine appearance and a beautiful, steady gait — large thighs, large face, a marked back, face or sides, a large hump on the neck. You are liberal in gifts, patient in nature, able to bear misfortunes, and endowed with great influence and authority; liked by all. You are a great eater, fond of women, attached to your friends. Happiness comes in middle and old age; therefore the young years ask for patience — the same steadiness that matures late is what makes the later life secure. There is a phlegmatic tendency to tend; daughters are indicated; separation from kinsmen, wealth or sons may occur and is met with the patience that is your gift.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Taurus will be of fine appearance and of beautiful gait; will possess large thighs and face; will possess marks on his back, face or sides; will be liberal in his gifts; will bear misfortunes; will possess great influence and authority; will have a large hump on the neck; will have daughters; will suffer from phlegmatic affections; will be separated from his kinsmen, wealth and sons; will be liked by all men; will be of patient nature; will be a great eater; will be fond of women; will be attached to his friends and will be happy both in middle age and old age (a)."

## R-17.3 — Moon in Gemini

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 3
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Gemini (Mithuna)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A sharp intellect and sweet speech — fond of women and skilled in sexual union, with red eyes, curling hairs, an elevated nose and beautiful features. You are learned in the Sastras, skilled in wit, in reading the thoughts of other men, and in games; skilled in music and the rules of dance; a carrier of messages. You are a great eater. The same mercurial versatility that makes you a fine communicator and artist also inclines you toward varied company, including union with hermaphrodites — a breadth of intimacy to hold with discernment rather than a defect.

## R-17.4 — Moon in Cancer

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 4
- **Classification:** neutral
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Cancer (Karka)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A receptive, feeling nature — you walk fast with the body bent, with a high hip, a short stature and a thick neck. You are moved by kind words and attached to your friends; you are fond of water and forests. Women hold influence over you, and you keep at least one excellent friend. You are learned in astrology. Your wealth rises and falls with the Moon — cyclic, not steady — and numerous houses come to you. The sensitivity that makes you winnable by gentleness is also what makes you a good friend and a student of the celestial order.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Cancer will walk fast with his body bent; will have a high hip; will be subject to the influence of women; will have an excellent friend; will be learned in Astrology; will have numerous houses; will have a wealth which will increase and decrease as the Moon; will be short; will possess a thick neck; will be capable of being won over by kind words; will be attached to his friends and will be fond of water and forests."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR corrections: "fl,lrests" → "forests". Moon is lord of Cancer, so by verse 13 the effects manifest fully when the sign-lord (Moon itself) is powerful.

## R-17.5 — Moon in Leo

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 5
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Leo (Simha)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A regal, fiery temperament — broad face, large cheeks, brown eyes; fond of animal food, forests and hills; haughty and attached to your mother; of fixed principles, liberal in gifts, and bold in fight. The same irascible temper that holds anger at trifles for a long time is the steadiness that makes your principles fixed and your courage reliable. You are afflicted with hunger, thirst, stomach-ache, tooth-ache and mental anxieties — these are the body's signal to tend the fire rather than let it burn unchecked. Sons are few; a distaste for women is noted. Hold the lion's dignity with the liberality that is your gift and the anger becomes guardianship rather than havoc.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Leo will be of irascible temper, of large cheeks, broad face and brown eyes; will have very few sons; will hate women; will be fond of animal food, forests and hills; will continue angry at trifles for a long time; will be afflicted with pains caused by hunger, thirst, stomach-ache, tooth-ache as well as purely mental anxieties; will be liberal in his gifts; will be bold in fight; will be of fixed principles; will be haughty and attached to his mother."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR corrections: "thir st" → "thirst"; "anxietie s" → "anxieties". The harsh traits (irascible, few sons, hate women, pains) are paired inline with the offsetting strengths (liberal, bold, fixed principles, attached to mother).

## R-17.6 — Moon in Virgo

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 6
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Virgo (Kanya)

## R-17.7 — Moon in Libra

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 7
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Libra (Tula)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A cultivated, principled, devotional nature — you respect the Devas, Brahmins and holy men; are intelligent (Pragna — acute intelligence knowing current and future events); learned in the Vedas; never covet the property of other men; rich; a trader; tall with a raised nose; protector of your family. You bear a name of a Deva coupled with an excellent surname granted by a body of learned men. The delicate side — thin and defective limbs, fondness for travel, sickness, subjection to women's influence, and being disgraced and rejected by kinsmen — is paired with the offsetting strengths: riches, learning, a conferred honour-name, and a protective role toward your family. Hold to the honour and the learning; the rejection by kinsmen is met by the body of learned men who grant your name.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Libra will respect the Devas, Brahmins, and holy men; will be intelligent (a), will never covet the property of other men; will be learned in the Vedas; will be subject to the influence of women; will be tall; will have a raised nose; will be of thin and defective limbs and fond of travels; will be rich; will be a trader; will bear the name of a Deva coupled with an excellent surname granted by a body of learned men; will be sickly; will protect his family and will be disgraced and rejected by his kinsmen."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator's footnote (a): "The Sanskrit term used is Pragna which is interpreted to mean a person of very acute intelligence and one that possesses a knowledge of the current and future events." OCR corrections: "libra" → "Libra"; "('J)" → "(a)".

## R-17.8 — Moon in Scorpio

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 8
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** Moon in rasi Scorpio (Vrischika)
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A strong, guarded, intense nature — broad eyes, broad breast, round shanks, thighs and knees; brown in colour. You are respected by the king's family. You are not straightforward and endeavour to conceal your sins; your palm and foot lines take the shape of the fish, the Vajrayudha, or a bird. Separation from parents and preceptors, and diseases in youth, are the early costs of this depth; the same guardedness that conceals also protects, and the respect of the royal family shows the weight you carry. Tend the secrecy toward discretion rather than concealment of wrong, and the intensity becomes discernment.
- **Verse (source):** "A person born with the Moon in sign Scorpio will have broad eyes, a broad breast and round shanks, thighs and knees; will be separated from his parents and preceptors; will be afflicted with diseases when young; will be respected by the king's family; will be of brown colour; will not be of a straightforward nature; will have lines in his hand and feet of the shape of the fish, the Vajrayudha or a bird; and will endeavour to conceal his sins."

## R-17.10 — Moon in Capricorn

You may be liberal, powerful, and a literary author. The shadow side — doing deeds of virtue for outward show, miserliness, shamelessness, mercilessness, and attachment to old women of low caste — is paired with the offsetting capacity for liberality, power, and authorship. ## R-17.13 — Strength qualification for sign-effects

- **Cites:** Ch 17, v 13
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** At birth, evaluate the power of (1) the Moon, (2) the sign occupied by the Moon, and (3) the lord of that sign. The same scheme applies to planets other than the Moon (per footnote b, applied in the next chapter).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The sign-effects of this chapter come to pass fully when all three — the planet, its sign, and the sign's lord — are powerful. If two of the three are powerful, the effects come to pass imperfectly; if only one is powerful, still more imperfectly; if none is powerful, the effects fail. This is the dial by which the per-sign readings above are tuned: it sets the volume, not the content, of each rule.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth of a person, the Moon, the sign occupied by the Moon and the lord of such sign be all powerful, the effects described in this chapter will fully come to pass (a); the same remark applies to planets other than the Moon (b)."
