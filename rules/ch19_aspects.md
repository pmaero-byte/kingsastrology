# Chapter 4 — Drishti — Planetary Aspects (effects of planets aspecting the Moon)

- **Source:** Brihat Jataka, Chapter XIX (numbered "ON PLANETARY ASPECTS" in the translation; project label Ch 4)
- **Source text:** sourcetext/pdfcoffee.com_brihat-jataka-of-varahamihira-pdf-free.pdf
- **Runtime use:** deterministic, one-way; no AI at runtime
- **Authoring:** drafted (AI-assisted) → human scholar review → frozen
- **Status legend:** see §5 of rules/README.md

> **Chapter scope.** This chapter gives a fixed lookup: when the Moon occupies a given
> sign (verses 1–3), a given Hora / Drekkana / Dwadasamsa context (verse 4), or a given
> Navamsa (verses 5–8), and is aspected by one of the six non-lunar planets
> (Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn), a stated effect follows. Verse 9 gives
> the strength gradient (full / imperfect / small) by Navamsa dignity, and a dominance
> rule when the Navamsa lord is powerful.
>
> Per the translator's note to verse 4, "all that has been said of the Moon applies also
> to the rising sign." This applies to every table rule below (R-4.1 … R-4.12 and the
> Navamsa rules R-4.17 … R-4.23): the same effect is read when the *rising sign*
> (or rising Navamsa) is the one named and it is aspected as stated, with the one
> exception that where the text says "Solar aspect" it is read as "Lunar aspect" for
> the rising-sign case (translator's note to verse 8).
>
> Unfavorable entries (thief, poor, deceitful, wicked deeds, "fond of the wives of other
> men," etc.) are **tendencies to tend**, not fate. Each table rule below pairs them with
> the dignified reading that the same sign/navamsa also produces under a benefic
> aspect, so the user is shown the strength first and the tendency as something to
> tend away from.

## R-19.1 — Moon in Aries aspected by the six planets

- **Cites:** Ch 19, v 1 (Aries portion)
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** Moon occupies sign Aries AND exactly one of {Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun} aspects the Moon (and no other of the six simultaneously aspects it, per the chapter's one-aspect-at-a-time reading).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):**
    - **Mars aspects → king.** A gift of command and self-mastery; use it to lead, not to dominate.
    - **Mercury aspects → learned.** A gift of study and articulate intelligence.
    - **Jupiter aspects → resembles a king.** Dignified bearing and wisdom that earns royal regard.
    - **Venus aspects → person of good conduct** (or, per another reading, a trader). Cultivate courtesy and fair dealing; commerce suits this placement.
    - **Saturn aspects → thief.** A tendency to take short-cuts or what is not freely given; tend it toward honest earning, which the same Aries-Moon placement readily supports under a benefic aspect.
    - **Sun aspects → poor.** A tendency toward financial strain; tend it by steady discipline, not pride-driven spending.
- **Verse (source):** "If at the time of birth, the Moon occupy sign Aries and be aspected by Mars, the person will become a king; if she be aspected by Mercury, the person will be learned; if by Jupiter, he will resemble a king; if by Venus, he will be a man of good conduct; or a trader according to another reading; if by Saturn, he will be a thief and if by the Sun, he will be poor."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR corrections: "wiII"→"will", "Jupit er"→"Jupiter", "wrtt"→"will", "k.ing"→"king", "accordin g"→"according", "anothe r"→"another". Per the chapter note (translator's note to v 4), the same effects apply when Aries is the rising sign aspected by these planets, with "Solar aspect" read as "Lunar aspect" in the rising-sign case.
