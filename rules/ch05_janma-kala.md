## R-4.1 — Father absent at birth; his location by sign nature

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 1
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, the rising sign (Lagna) is not aspected by the Moon, AND the Sun occupies the 9th or the 8th house from Lagna. The nature of that house's sign (movable / fixed / common) determines the father's location.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The father is away from the birthplace at the time of delivery. If the occupied house is a movable sign, he is in a foreign country; if fixed, he is in his own country; if common (dual), he is on his way back to his native country. This is a description of distance, not a judgment on the bond — the father's connection to the child is undiminished by geography, and many families today are healthily mobile.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth of a child the rising sign be not aspected by the Moon the father will not be near when the child is born—and if in such a case, the Sun should occupy the 9th or the 8th house, the father would be in a foreign country if the house be a movable sign; in his own country if the house be a fixed sign; and would be returning to his country if the house be a common sign."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** OCR corrections: "aspeeled" → "aspected", "sian" → "sign". Testable from chart: Lagna, Moon aspect on Lagna, Sun's house, sign nature (movable/fixed/common).

## R-4.2 — Father not near; three lagna/malefic conditions

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 2
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, any one of: (a) Saturn in the rising sign (Lagna); (b) Mars in the setting sign (7th house); (c) the Moon is between Mercury and Venus (i.e. Mercury and Venus occupy the signs on both sides of the Moon, or the same sign as the Moon — see Notes).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The father is not physically near the child at the moment of birth. This is a placement reading, not a verdict on paternal presence through life; the father-child relationship is built across years, and distance at one moment does not define it.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, Saturn be in the rising sign or Mars in the setting sign, or the Moon between (a) Mercury and Venus, the father will not be near."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator note (a): "Whether Mercury and Venus occupy the signs on both sides of the Moon or occupy the same sign as the Moon." The "between" condition needs scholar review to pin down the exact testable definition (planetary longitude ordering vs. sign-adjacency).

## R-4.3 — Serpent-coiled birth; Mars Drekkana rising with benefics in 2nd/11th

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 3
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, the Moon is in a Drekkana of Mars (1st Drekkana of Aries, 2nd of Cancer, 3rd of Leo, 1st of Scorpio, 2nd of Sagittarius, 3rd of Pisces) AND benefic planets occupy the 2nd and 11th houses from the rising sign. Variant: the rising sign itself is a malefic sign (Aries, Cancer under waning Moon, Leo, Scorpio) and a Mars Drekkana is rising, with benefics in 2nd and 11th.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The classical reading describes the newborn as accompanied by a serpent or born with a serpent coiled round the body — symbolic of an unusual presentation or omen at birth. The presence of benefics in the 2nd and 11th is itself a protective, resourceful signature; whatever the birth circumstance, it points toward sustenance and guardianship around the child.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, the Moon be in the Drekkana of Mars (a) and if benefic planets be in the 2nd and 11th houses from the rising sign, the issue will be a serpent. If the sign (b) of a malefic planet be the ascendant sign, and if the Drekkana of Mars should then be rising and if benefic planets occupy the 2nd and the 11th houses the issue will be a child born with a serpent coiled round its body."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator note (a) lists the six Mars Drekkanas. Note (b) lists the malefic signs: Aries, Cancer (under waning Moon), Leo, Scorpio. The literal "issue will be a serpent" is preserved verbatim per spec; effect text re-frames symbolically. Flag for scholar review on interpretation.

## R-4.5 — Umbilical cord twined; Saturn/Mars in Aries, Leo, or Taurus lagna

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 5
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, the rising sign is Aries, Leo, or Taurus, AND Saturn or Mars occupies that rising sign. The body part affected follows the rising Navamsa (Aries Navamsa → head; Taurus Navamsa → face; and so on per the body divisions of Ch 1, v 4).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The classical reading is that the umbilical cord is twined round the body part indicated by the rising Navamsa at delivery. This is a description of a birth presentation detail, not a lasting condition; modern delivery care readily manages cord presentations, and the cord itself is the child's first lifeline.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, Aries, Leo or Taurus be the rising sign, and if Saturn or Mars be in such sign, the issue will be a child born with the umbilical cord twined round the part of body indicated by the rising Navamsa (a)."

## R-4.6 — Illegitimate birth; Jupiter not aspecting Lagna and Moon

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 6
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, Jupiter fails to aspect BOTH the ascendant sign AND the Moon; OR the Moon is in conjunction with the Sun; OR the Moon is conjunct the Sun when the Sun is accompanied by a malefic planet. Exemption (Notes): if Jupiter aspects either the Lagna or the Moon, the child is not illegitimate; per Yavaneswara, if the Lagna Navamsa or the Moon's Navamsa is Jupiter's, the child is not illegitimate; per Garga, if the Moon is in a sign of Jupiter, or conjunct Jupiter in any other sign, or in the Drekkana/Navamsa of Jupiter, the child is not illegitimate.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The classical verse addresses circumstances of parentage. The offsetting exemptions are explicit and broad: Jupiter's aspect on either Lagna or Moon, or Jupiter's Navamsa/sign/Drekkana linkage to the Moon, removes the indication entirely. Where the offsetting Jovian grace is present, the reading does not apply.
- **Verse (source):** "If Jupiter, at the time of birth, fail to aspect both the ascendant sign and the Moon (a) or the Moon when in conjunction with the Sun, or if the Moon be in conjunction with the Sun when he is accompanied by a malefic planet, the issue will be an illegitimate child."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator note (a): "If Jupiter aspect either the one or the other, the child will not be legitimate" (the translator's wording here is itself ambiguous — likely means "will not be illegitimate"; flagged for scholar review). Alternate readings from Yavaneswara and Garga recorded above. This rule is sensitive; the effect text is kept restrained and the exemptions foregrounded. Scholar review required before any runtime use.

## R-4.7 — Father in custody; two malefics in malefic sign 5th/7th/9th from Sun

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 7
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, two malefic planets (Mars and Saturn) occupy one of the malefic signs (Aries, Leo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Aquarius, Cancer under waning Moon, Gemini and Virgo when Mercury is conjunct a malefic), AND that sign is the 5th, 7th, or 9th house from the Sun. The Sun's own sign-nature (movable/fixed/common) refines the father's location.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The classical reading is that the father is in custody at the time of birth. If the Sun occupies a movable sign, this is in foreign lands; if fixed, in his own country; if common, he is on his way back to his native country. This is a placement-of-father reading for that moment; it does not foreclose the father's future, and a single configuration at birth does not determine a man's whole story.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, two malefic planets (a) occupy one of the malefic signs (b) and if such sign be the 5th, 7th, or the 9th house from the Sun, the father of the child will be in custody at the time. If the Sun occupy a movable sign, the father will be so in foreign lands; if he occupy a fixed sign, the father will be in custody in his own country; and if he occupy a common sign, the father will be on his way to his native country."

## R-4.10 — Birth in a prison or a ditch

## R-4.12 — Birth place by aspecting planet on biped lagna with Saturn

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 12
- **Classification:** neutral
- **Condition:** At birth, a biped (human) sign rises AND Saturn occupies that rising sign AND Saturn is aspected by one of: Mars; Venus and the Moon together; Jupiter; the Sun; Mercury. The aspecting planet(s) determine the place.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The birthplace follows the aspecting planet: Mars → cremation ground; Venus and the Moon → a beautiful and agreeable spot; Jupiter → the Agnihotra sala (the place where the sacred fire is kept and worshipped); the Sun → the King's palace, a temple, or a cow shed; Mercury → an artist's working room. - **Verse (source):** "If a biped sign be rising, and if Saturn occupy it and be aspected by Mars, the birth will be in cremation ground; if aspected by Venus and the Moon, the birth will occur in some beautiful and agreeable spot; if aspected by Jupiter, then in the Agnihotra sala (a);—if aspected by the Sun, then in the King's palace or a temple or a cow shed; and if aspected by Mercury, the birth will occur in an artist's working room."

## R-4.13 — Birth place by rising sign / Navamsa; place-sign table

If the rising Navamsa is Vargottama (same sign as the rasi) → birth in the mother's own house. The specific place follows the sign's nature per the table below.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** The birthplace is read from the more powerful of the rising sign or Navamsa, refined by movable/fixed/Vargottama status, and further specified by the place-sign table (Aries → mountain caves, mines, army residences, fireplaces; Taurus → forests, cattle grounds, farmers' dwellings; Gemini → places of music, painting, and love-message carrying; Cancer → rice fields, tanks, sand banks; Leo → forests, caves, mountain forest lands; Virgo → grassy places, women's quarters, female schools; Libra → customs houses, bazaars, market roads, high ground; Scorpio → caves, fortified towns, ditches, snake/scorpion holes; Sagittarius → even grounds of horses, cavalry, armed men, sacrificial fire, vehicles; Capricorn → rivers, gardens, forests, tanks, water banks, ditches; Aquarius → places of birds, women, liquor-dealers, gamblers; Pisces → temples, Brahmins' places, holy waters, rivers, seas).

## R-4.14 — Child deserted by mother; Mars+Saturn with Sun/Moon in trines

## R-4.15 — Deserted child's fate; malefic/benefic power on the Moon

Outcomes depend on the Moon's additional aspects: if the Moon is aspected by a powerful benefic, the deserted child falls into the hands of the class of men represented by that benefic and lives; if the aspecting malefic is powerful, the child falls into others' hands and perishes; if Jupiter aspects the Moon (per note c), none of these evils befall the child.

If the Moon be also aspected by a powerful benefic planet (c) the deserted child will fall into the hands of the class of men represented by the aspecting benefic planet and will live. ## R-4.17 — Dark spot / bare ground / presentation / mother's travail

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 17
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At birth, the Moon occupies the Navamsa of Saturn, OR the 4th house (Kendra — 1st/4th/7th/10th per Yavanacharya) from Lagna, OR is aspected by Saturn, OR occupies a watery Navamsa (Cancer or Pisces Navamsa), OR occupies the same house as Saturn → birth in a dark spot (offset note c: if the Sun is powerful and aspected by Mars, there is no darkness). Separately, if three or more planets are in their Neecha (depression) signs → birth on bare ground (or ground covered with straw, per Commentator; offset per Saravali: if the Moon is in Lagna or 4th and in her depression sign, also bare ground). The birth presentation follows how the rising sign reaches the horizon (head-first / leg-first / hands-first). - **Verse (source):** "If the Moon occupy the Navamsa of Saturn or the 4th house (a) from the Lagna, or if she be aspected by Saturn, or if she occupy a watery Navamsa (b) or the same house as Saturn, the birth will occur in a dark spot (c). If three or more planets be in their Neecha signs (d), the birth will occur on the bare ground (e). The birth will follow the manner in which the rising sign reaches the horizon (f).
