## R-1.4 — Signs, Kalapurusha body parts & synonyms

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 4
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (foundational sign set: 12 signs from Aries, each of 9 nakshatra-padas, forming the circle)
- **Effect (one-way):** The twelve signs from Aries map to the body of Kalapurusha — Aries=head, Taurus=face, Gemini=breast, Cancer=heart, Leo=belly, Virgo=navel, Libra=abdomen, Scorpio=genital organ, Sagittarius=two thighs, Capricorn=two knees, Aquarius=two ankles, Pisces=two feet. The terms Rasi, Kshetra, Griha, Riksha, Bham, Bhavana are synonymous (all mean a sign = 30° of the ecliptic).
- **Verse (source):** "The (twelve) signs of the Zodiac, commencing with the first point of Aries and of (the asterism of) Aswini, and consisting, each, of nine stellar quarters and forming a circle, are respectively the head, face, breast, heart, belly, navel, abdomen, genital organ, two thighs, two knees, two ankles and the two feet of Kalapurusha. (The terms) Rasi, Kshetra, Griha, Riksha, Bham, Bhavana, are synonymous terms."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Foundational. The 27 nakshatras and their pada→sign mapping (each sign = 9 padas) are part of this table; the nakshatra list is given in the source notes (Aswini…Revati).

## R-1.5 — Shapes of the signs

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 5
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (sign-shape lookup)
- **Effect:** Pisces = two fish lying head-to-tail; Aquarius = a man with a water-pot; Gemini = a man (with a stick) and a woman (with a lyre) seated together; Sagittarius = a man whose lower parts are a horse; Capricorn = a crocodile with a deer's face; Libra = a person holding scales; Virgo = a virgin in a boat with crops and a light. Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Leo, Scorpio resemble the creatures of their names (ram, bull, crab, lion, scorpion). Each sign's dwelling place matches its creature.
- **Verse (source):** "Sign Pisces is of the shape of two fish; sign Aquarius is of the shape of a man with a (water) pot; sign Gemini is of the shape of a man and woman … sign Sagittari is of the shape of a man who in his lower parts is a horse; sign Capricorn is of the shape of a crocodile with the face of a deer; sign Libra is of the shape of a person with scales (in his hand); and sign Virgo is of the shape of a virgin in a boat with crops (in one hand) and a light (in the other). The other signs resemble in shape the different creatures denoted by their names."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Source notes these shapes are chiefly useful in horary (discovery of stolen property). Neutral/descriptive.

## R-1.6 — Sign lords & Navamsa/Dwadasamsa lords

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 6
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (sign-lord lookup)
- **Effect:** The lords of the twelve signs from Aries are: Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn, Jupiter. The same lords govern the Navamsas and Dwadasamsas. The four sign-sets begin their Navamsas with: Aries-set (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) → Navamsa of Aries; Taurus-set (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) → Navamsa of Capricorn; Gemini-set (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) → Navamsa of Libra; Cancer-set (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) → Navamsa of Cancer.
- **Verse (source):** "Mars, Venus, Mercury, the Moon, the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn and Jupiter are respectively the lords of the Signs, and of the Navamsas and Dwadasamsas; and the several signs (of the Zodiac beginning from Aries) commence respectively with the Navamsas of Mesha, Makara, Tula and Kataka."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical lookup used by dignity, yoga, and dasa rules. Sun & Moon each own one sign; the other five planets each own two (Swakshetra): Mars=Aries&Scorpio, Venus=Taurus&Libra, Mercury=Gemini&Virgo, Jupiter=Sagittarius&Pisces, Saturn=Capricorn&Aquarius; Moon=Cancer, Sun=Leo.

## R-1.7 — Trimsamsa divisions

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 7
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (Trimsamsa lord lookup)
- **Effect:** In odd signs, the Trimsamsa (30 parts of 1° each) belong to: Mars (first 5°), Saturn (next 5°), Jupiter (next 8°), Mercury (next 7°), Venus (last 5°). In even signs the order is reversed: Venus (5°), Mercury (7°), Jupiter (8°), Saturn (5°), Mars (5°). The ends (last Navamsas) of Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces are Riksha-sandhis.
- **Verse (source):** "Five, five, eight, seven and five parts (degrees) are respectively those of Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury and Venus in the odd signs. In the even signs their order is reversed. The ends (last Navamsas) of Kataka, Vrischika and Meena are known as Riksha sandhis."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Trimsamsa is used by the author chiefly in the horoscopy of women (source Ch XXIV). Odd signs: Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius. Even: Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces.

## R-1.9 — The six Vargas

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 9
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (varga definitions)
- **Effect:** A planet is "in its Varga" when it occupies its own Drekkana, Hora, Navamsa, Trimsamsa, Dwadasamsa and Kshetra. Hora = both the rising sign and one-half of a sign (15°). The Shadvarga are: Rasi (12×30°), Hora (2×15°), Drekkana (3×10°), Navamsa (9×3°20'), Dwadasamsa (12×2°30'), Trimsamsa (30×1°).
- **Verse (source):** "A planet is said to be in its Varga if it be in its Drekkana, Hora, Navamsa, Trimsamsa, Dwadasamsa and Kshetra. The term Hora means both the rising sign and one-half of a sign of the Zodiac."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** A Navamsa equals one Nakshatra-pada (1/4 of a lunar mansion); 108 Navamsas and 108 padas coincide. The Dwadasamsas of a sign bear the 12 sign-names starting from that sign.

## R-1.10 — Day/night signs & mode of rising

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 10
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (sign day/night + rising-mode lookup)
- **Effect:** Night signs: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn; except Gemini they rise by their feet (Prishtodaya) and are powerful at night. Day signs (Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius, Pisces) rise by their heads (Sirodaya) and are powerful by day. Pisces rises by both head and foot.
- **Verse (source):** "The signs Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Sagittari and Capricorn are known as the night signs; and, with the exception of Gemini, they rise with their feet. The other signs rise with their heads and are powerful by day. The two fish rise by both (head and foot)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Prishtodaya = Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn. Sirodaya = Gemini, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius.

## R-1.11 — Sign qualities (gender/nature/mobility), directions, Hora & Drekkana lords

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 11
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (sign-quality lookup)
- **Effect:** Signs from Aries alternate malefic/benefic, masculine/feminine, and movable/fixed/common. Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer (with their trikona signs) denote East, South, West, North respectively. The two Horas of odd signs are Solar then Lunar; of even signs Lunar then Solar. The lords of the three Drekkanas of a sign are respectively the lords of that sign, the 5th sign, and the 9th sign.
- **Verse (source):** "The signs (from Aries) are (by turns) malefic and benefic, masculine and feminine and movable, fixed and both (movable and fixed). The signs Aries, Taurus, Gemini and Cancer with their triangular signs denote each respectively the East, South, West and North. The two horas of the odd signs are respectively the Solar and Lunar horas, and the two of the even signs are respectively the Lunar and Solar horas. The lords of the Drekkanas, (of a particular sign) are respectively the lords of that sign, the 5th sign and the 9th sign."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Odd signs = malefic & masculine; even = benefic & feminine. Movable: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Fixed: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Common (both): Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces. Direction trikonas: East=Aries/Leo/Sagittarius; South=Taurus/Virgo/Capricorn; West=Gemini/Libra/Aquarius; North=Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces.

## R-1.13 — Exaltation & depression signs and degrees  *(critical)*

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 13
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (dignity lookup)
- **Effect:** Exaltation (Uccha) signs and degrees of deepest exaltation: Sun=Aries 10°, Moon=Taurus 3°, Mars=Capricorn 28°, Mercury=Virgo 15°, Jupiter=Cancer 5°, Venus=Pisces 27°, Saturn=Libra 20°. The 7th (opposite) sign is the depression (Neecha) sign, with the same degree of deepest depression. A planet in exaltation is exceedingly powerful; in depression exceedingly weak.
- **Verse (source):** "The signs Aries, Taurus, Capricorn, Virgo, Cancer, Pisces and Libra are respectively the Uccha (exaltation) signs of the Sun and other planets. The 10th, 3rd, 28th, 15th, 5th, 27th, and 20th are the degrees of main exaltation of the several planets. The 7th or opposite signs are the Neecha (depression) signs; and the degrees of main depression are the same as given above."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical — used by every strength/dignity rule (Ch II stanzas 19–21) and by Raja Yoga etc.

## R-1.14 — Vargottama & Moolatrikona  *(critical)*

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 14
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (dignity lookup)
- **Effect:** Vargottama positions (planet produces good effects): the 1st Navamsa of movable signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn), the 5th Navamsa of fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius), and the 9th Navamsa of common signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) — these Navamsas bear the same name as the sign itself. Moolatrikona houses: Sun=Leo, Moon=Taurus, Mars=Aries, Mercury=Virgo, Jupiter=Sagittarius, Venus=Libra, Saturn=Aquarius.
- **Verse (source):** "In the movable and other signs, the first, the central and the last Navamsas are known as Vargottama positions. Planets in such positions will produce good effects. The signs Leo, Taurus, Aries, Virgo, Sagittari, Libra, and Aquarius are known as the Moola Trikona houses of the planets respectively."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical dignity table. Vargottama = same sign in Rasi and Navamsa.

## R-1.15 — House significations & Upachaya  *(critical)*

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 15
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (bhava signification lookup)
- **Effect:** The 12 houses from the ascendant signify: 1=body, 2=family, 3=brothers, 4=relations, 5=sons, 6=enemies, 7=wife, 8=death, 9=deeds of virtue, 10=avocation, 11=gain, 12=loss. The 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th from the ascendant are the Upachaya (improving) signs.
- **Verse (source):** "The significations of the 12 houses from the rising sign (ascendant) are respectively (a native's) body, family, brothers, relations, sons, enemies, wife, death, deed of virtue, avocation, gain and loss. The 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th houses from the ascendant are known as the Upachaya (improving) signs, but not so according to some."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Critical — the bhava framework. Expanded in source notes: 1=body/fame/limbs; 2=family/wealth/eyes/speech/truthfulness; 3=brothers/bravery/meals; 4=relations/education/mother/dwelling/comfort; 5=sons/intelligence/previous karma; 6=enemies/diseases; 7=wife/generosity/respect; 8=death/duration of life; 9=virtue/father/medicine; 10=avocation/knowledge/clothes; 11=gain/earning; 12=loss/bad deeds/travels. Garga's school holds Upachaya cease if afflicted by malefics/unfriendly lords — the Author (with Satyacharya, Yavaneswara) does not agree.

## R-1.16 — Technical names of the twelve houses

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 16
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (house-name lookup)
- **Effect:** The 12 houses from the ascendant are technically: 1=Kalpa (power), 2=Sva (wealth), 3=Vikrama (prowess), 4=Ohila (dwelling), 5=Pratibha (intelligence), 6=Kshata (wound), 7=Manmatha (desire), 8=Randra (hole), 9=Guru (father/preceptor), 10=Mana (respectability), 11=Bhava (acquisition), 12=Vyaya (loss). 4th & 8th = Chaturasra; 7th = Dyuna; 10th = Agna.
- **Verse (source):** "The twelve signs of the Zodiac beginning from the ascendant are known technically as the signs of (1) Kalpa (power), (2) Sva (wealth), (3) Vikrama (prowess), (4) Ohila (dwelling place), (5) Pratibha (intelligence), (6) Kshata (wound), (7) Manmatha (desire), (8) Randra (hole), (9) Guru (father or preceptor), (10) Mana (respectability), (11) Bhava (acquisition), (12) Vyaya (loss). The 4th and 8th houses are known as Chaturasra signs. The 7th house is known as Dyuna and the 10th house is known as Agna."

## R-1.17 — Kendra houses & their strength by creature-class

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 17
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (Kendra lookup)
- **Effect:** The 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses are the Kendra (Kantaka / Chatuhtaya — quadrants). These four Kendras possess strength when they are respectively biped, aquatic, centiped and quadruped. Biped signs: Gemini, Libra, Virgo, first half of Sagittarius, Aquarius. Aquatic: Cancer, second half of Capricorn, Pisces. Centiped: Scorpio. Quadruped: Aries, Taurus, Leo, second half of Sagittarius, first half of Capricorn.
- **Verse (source):** "The 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses are known as Kantaka or Kendra or Chatushtaya houses (quadrants or angles). If these four houses should happen to be biped, aquatic, centiped and quadruped respectively, they then possess strength."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Creature-class table used by Kendra-strength rules (R-1.19) and yoga rules.

## R-1.18 — Panapara, Apoklima & house technical names

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 18
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** always (house-group lookup)
- **Effect:** Houses next to the angles = Panapara (2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th); those next to them = Apoklima (3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th). 4th house = Hibuka / Ambu / Sukha / Vesma. 7th = Jamitra. 5th = Trikona. 10th = Meshoorana / Karma.
- **Verse (source):** "The houses next to the angles are known as Panapara houses and those next to them are known as Apoklima houses. The 4th house is known as Hibuka, Ambu, Sukha and Vesma. The 7th house is known as Jamitra. The 5th house is known as Trikona. The 10th house is known as Meshoorana and Karma."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Panapara & Apoklima are Greek-derived terms (source note).

## R-1.19 — Strength of the ascendant, Kendras & signs by time; sign "Mana" (size)

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 19
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** Lagna aspected/occupied by its lord OR Jupiter OR Mercury → Lagna powerful (not so by other planets). Kendra signs naturally powerful. Biped signs powerful by day; quadruped at night; centiped at sunrise & sunset.
- **Effect:** The ascendant gains strength when occupied or aspected by the lord of the ascendant, Jupiter, or Mercury — and not by other planets. Kendra signs are naturally powerful. Among the Kendras: biped signs are powerful in the 1st Kendra, quadruped in the 10th, centiped in the 7th, aquatic in the 4th. The "Mana" (measurement) of the first six signs from Aries is 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40; of the next six 40, 36, 32, 28, 24, 20. (Panapara < Kendra in power; Apoklima weakest.) 3rd house = Duschikhya; 9th = Tapas / Tritrikona.
- **Verse (source):** "If the lord of the ascendant or Jupiter or Mercury should either occupy it or aspect it, the ascendant (Lagna) would become powerful and not by other planets. The Kendra signs are naturally powerful. The biped signs are powerful during the day, the quadruped signs at night, and the centiped signs at sunrise and at sunset. The Mana (measurement) of the first six signs from Aries are 20, 24, 28, 32, 36 and 40, respectively; and those of the next six signs are 40, 36, 32, 28, 24 and 20 respectively. The 3rd house is known as Duschikhya and the 9th as Tapas and Tritrikona."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** The "Mana" values feed oblique-ascension/time computations (cross-ref Ch V). Foundational for Shadbala-style positional strength.

## R-1.20 — Colors of the signs; Plava; Vesi

- **Cites:** Ch 1, v 20

The signs are known as Plava (depressed) in the direction of their lords. The 2nd house to that occupied by the Sun is known as Vesi."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Color/direction table; Vesi definition feeds the Chandra-yoga / solar-position yogas (Ch XIII). Plava used in Ch V (stanza 21).
