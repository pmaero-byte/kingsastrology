# Chapter 4 — Double Yogas — Planetary Yogas from Pairs Conjoined in a Sign

> Note on chapter numbering: the source page-headers read "Ch. XIV" for this chapter, but the project indexes it as Chapter 4 (Double Yogas). Verses are cited as Ch 4 per the project assignment; verse numbers follow the translator's "1."…"5." markers.

> Framing note (Pillar 3): The verse-text describes occupational, character, and circumstantial fruits of two planets occupying a single sign together. In keeping with the one-way, dignity-closing, kind voice of this product, effects below render these as *tendencies and capacities* — gifts to develop, cautions to tend — not literal destinies. Where the verse carries a harsh fruit (cruelty, deceit, grief, low trade), the rule is classified `tend` and paired with an offsetting strength or constructive frame; the reader is pointed toward the dignified use of the same combination.

> Scope note: This chapter's core verses (v 1–5) cover all 21 unordered pairs of the seven non-Lagna planets {Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn} conjoined in one sign. The translator appends supplementary yogas for groups of 3, 4, 5, and 6 planets taken from the Jataka Parijata (a different work); those multi-planet yogas are outside the "double planetary yoga" scope of this file and are not authored here as separate rules, but the splitting method given in verse 5 note (a) is captured as R-4.22. The Jataka Parijata's alternate two-planet readings are preserved in each rule's Notes as alternate readings.

## R-14.1 — Sun and Moon conjoined

- **Cites:** Ch 14, v 1
- **Classification:** neutral
- **Condition:** The Sun and the Moon occupy the same sign (conjunction, any sign, any house).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** When the Sun and Moon share a sign, the native's craft lies in fire and stone — the work of engines and of masonry. This is a maker's temperament: hands that shape hard materials and tend flame. The native is invited to develop this craft honestly, turning the commentator's "engines of destruction" into engines of useful work.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of birth, the Sun and the Moon occupy together a sign of the Zodiac, the person born will be a maker of fire engines (a) and will work in stones"
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator note (a): "Engines of destruction according to the Commentator." Jataka Parijata alternate reading (Sun+Moon): "the person will be subject to the influence of women, will be skilled in work and will be of bright appearance." OCR corrections: "tbe" → "the".

## R-14.22 — Splitting multi-planet yogas into double planetary yogas

- **Cites:** Ch 14, v 5, note (a)
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** When three or more planets occupy a single sign together at birth, split the conjunction into all its unordered pairs and evaluate each pair as a separate double planetary yoga per R-4.1 through R-4.21. The set of pairs is the set of all 2-combinations of the planets sharing the sign. Example: Sun, Moon, Mars, and Mercury together in one sign yields six double yogas — (Sun,Moon), (Sun,Mars), (Sun,Mercury), (Moon,Mars), (Moon,Mercury), (Mars,Mercury).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** When several planets gather in one sign, the reading is built by combining the effects of each pair among them, per the double-yoga rules above. The verse closes by stating that for other planetary yogas (three or more together), the effects described in this chapter are to be determined and applied in this pairwise manner.
- **Verse (source):** "In the case of other planetary yogas (a), the effects described shall be determined and applied."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Translator note (a): "That is, if three or more planets occupy together a sign of the Zodiac at the time of birth, the yoga shall first be split into a number of double planetary yogas and the effects described in this Chapter shall then be applied. For example, suppose the four planets, the Sun, the Moon, Mars and Mercury to occupy together a single sign. This yoga can be split into six double planetary yogas as follows: (1) the Sun and the Moon, (2) the Sun and Mars, (3) the Sun and Mercury, (4) the Moon and Mars, (5) the Moon and Mercury, (6) Mars and Mercury. The effects described for each shall be determined and applied." This is a method/table rule used by the engine to decompose multi-planet conjunctions; the pairwise combination count C(n,2) is deterministic. OCR corrections: "NOTF..S" → "NOTES"; "birih" → "birth"; "supp ose" → "suppose"; "thc" → "the"; "Mercur y" → "Mercury".
