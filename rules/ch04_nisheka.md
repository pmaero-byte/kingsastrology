> **Framing note for this chapter:** Every rule below concerns *favorable or unfavorable timing for undertaking the act of conception* (a muhurta/fitness reading), read from the chart of the moment. Effects are NEVER a medical fertility guarantee, diagnosis, or pronouncement over a person's body. The constructive framing for every `tend` rule is: choose a different timing window; the chart describes the moment, not the person.

## R-4.1 — Moon in Anupachaya sign → conception follows

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 1
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** The Moon is aspected by Mars (occupies the 4th, 7th, or 8th house from Mars) AND the Moon occupies an Anupachaya sign (any sign other than the 3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th from the ascendant) at the time the menses appear.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** This is a favorable window for undertaking conception — the monthly sign of fertility aligns with an Anupachaya placement of the Moon, the combination classical texts associate with conception following. A timely, gifted moment to act.
- **Verse (source):** "The menses that appear monthly, because of Mars and the Moon (a) bring about conception when the Moon is in one of the Anupachaya signs (b)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): menses appear when the Moon is aspected by Mars, especially from the 4th, 7th, or 8th house from Mars. Footnote (b): Upachaya signs are 3, 6, 10, 11 from ascendant; all others are Anupachaya. The Saravali quote on Moon=water, Mars=fire, bile+blood is in the translator's notes.

## R-4.2 — Moon in Upachaya, aspected by benefic male → union with husband

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 1
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** The Moon occupies an Upachaya sign (3rd, 6th, 10th, or 11th from the ascendant — i.e. "otherwise" than Anupachaya) AND the Moon is aspected by a benefic male planet (Jupiter, per the translator's footnote).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** This window favors sexual union between wife and husband. The benefic male aspect upon the Moon in an Upachaya sign marks a favorable, dignified timing for the couple's union.
- **Verse (source):** "If the Moon be otherwise (c) and be aspected by a benefic male planet (d), there will be sexual union between a woman and her husband."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (c): "otherwise" = from the 4th-day bath onward, when the Moon reaches an Upachaya house in the husband's horoscope. Footnote (d): the benefic male planet is Jupiter when powerful. The Commentator adds further associations (Sun→king's officer; Mars→voluptuary; Mercury→fickle person; Venus→beautiful person; Saturn→servant; several malefics→harlot) — these describe the *nature of the partner* in union; flagged for scholar review on whether to include as sub-rules.

## R-4.3 — Setting sign occupied/aspected by malefics → union attended with anger

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 2
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At the time of union, the setting sign (the sign setting on the western horizon) is occupied OR aspected by malefic planets (Sun, Mars, Saturn — per classical malefic set; here treated as the natural malefics).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Unfavorable timing for undertaking the union: the moment is colored by anger. The offsetting strength — when benefics instead occupy or aspect the setting sign (see R-4.4), the same moment turns to play and laughter. Choose the benefic-aspected window; the setting sign is the variable you can wait upon.
- **Verse (source):** "The sexual union will be of the nature of the union of creatures represented by the setting sign. Again, if at the time of sexual union, the setting sign be either occupied or aspected by malefic planets, such union will be attended with anger, and if by benefic planets, it will be attended with play and laughter."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** The Commentator adds: if the setting sign is occupied/aspected by BOTH malefic and benefic planets, the union is attended with both joy and displeasure.

## R-4.5 — Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars in own Navamsas, or Jupiter in 1/5/9 → child produced

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 3
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** At the time of union, EITHER (a) the Sun, the Moon, Venus, and Mars each occupy their own Navamsas; OR (b) Jupiter occupies the rising sign, or the 5th, or the 9th sign from it.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A favorable timing for undertaking conception toward a child: these planetary placements are the classical signature that the union will produce a child. To persons devoid of virility, the yoga is as useless as moonlight to the blind — the chart describes the moment's potential, not the person's body.
- **Verse (source):** "If, at the time of sexual union, the Sun, the Moon, Venus and Mars be in their Navamsas (a), or if Jupiter occupy the rising sign or the 5th or the 9th sign from it, such union will produce a child. To persons devoid of virility, the above Yoga (planetary positions) will be as useless as the rays of the Moon to the blind."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): the Commentator adds conception also occurs when these four are NOT in their Navamsas, IF (i) Sun and Venus occupy Upachaya signs in the man's horoscope and are in their Navamsas, OR (ii) Mars and the Moon occupy Upachaya signs in the woman's horoscope and are in their Navamsas. The "devoid of virility" clause is an explicit classical caveat that the yoga presupposes bodily capacity — the rule is about timing, not a medical guarantee.

## R-4.6 — Mars or Saturn in 7th from Sun or Moon → man/wife fall ill

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 4
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At the time of conception, either Mars OR Saturn occupies the 7th house from the Sun (→ man falls ill) OR the 7th house from the Moon (→ wife falls ill).
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Unfavorable timing for undertaking conception in this window: illness is signaled for the partner whose luminary (Sun=father/man, Moon=mother/wife) is flanked. The offsetting strength and constructive path — this is a timing to avoid, not a verdict on the couple; choosing a different muhurta when Mars/Saturn are clear of the 7th from the luminaries is the remedial move. The illness, where it does manifest, falls in the month of Mars or Saturn respectively (cross-reference R-4.16/R-4.17 month-lords).
- **Verse (source):** "If at the time of conception, either Mars or Saturn should occupy the seventh house from the Sun or the Moon, the man and his wife would respectively fall ill (a); but if one of the two planets, Mars and Jupiter, should occupy the twelfth and the other the second house from the Sun and the Moon, or if one of the planets should be in conjunction with the Sun or the Moon and the other should aspect either the Sun or the Moon, the man and his wife will respectively meet with death (b)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): in the month of Mars or Saturn as the case may be (vide Stanza 16). The Commentator states these effects occur BEFORE, not after, the birth of the child.

## R-4.7 — Mars/Jupiter in 12th & 2nd from Sun & Moon (or one conjunct, one aspecting) → death of man and wife

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 4
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At the time of conception, one of {Mars, Jupiter} occupies the 12th house and the other occupies the 2nd house, counted from the Sun AND the Moon; OR one of them is in conjunction with the Sun or the Moon and the other aspects either the Sun or the Moon.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** This is a strongly unfavorable timing for undertaking conception — the classical signature of danger to the lives of the man and wife respectively. The offsetting strength and constructive framing: this is a window to AVOID, not a sentence; the couple who wait for a muhurta free of this Mars-Jupiter flanking of the luminaries choose the dignified, life-protecting path. Where it manifests, it occurs in the month of whichever of Saturn/Mars is powerful (per the footnote).
- **Verse (source):** "If at the time of conception, either Mars or Saturn should occupy the seventh house from the Sun or the Moon, the man and his wife would respectively fall ill (a); but if one of the two planets, Mars and Jupiter, should occupy the twelfth and the other the second house from the Sun and the Moon, or if one of the planets should be in conjunction with the Sun or the Moon and the other should aspect either the Sun or the Moon, the man and his wife will respectively meet with death (b)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (b): in the month of either Saturn or Mars, whichever is powerful. Commentator: effects occur before, not after, the birth. Note the verse pairs Mars with Jupiter (not Saturn) for the death-yoga — flagged for scholar review, as this is an unusual pairing.

## R-4.8 — Day vs night conception: luminaries representing parents, and odd/even sign placement → happiness of parents/aunts/uncles

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 5
- **Classification:** table
- **Condition:** At conception by DAY: Sun = father, Venus = mother, Saturn = paternal uncle, Moon = maternal aunt. At conception by NIGHT: Saturn = father, Moon = mother, Sun = paternal uncle, Venus = maternal aunt. THEN: if the planets representing father & paternal uncle occupy ODD signs, OR the planets representing mother & maternal aunt occupy EVEN signs, the respective relatives are happy.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A descriptive timing table: by day the Sun and Venus speak for father and mother; by night Saturn and the Moon do. When the father/paternal-uncle planets fall in odd signs, or the mother/maternal-aunt planets in even signs, the moment carries a favorable signature of happiness for those relatives.
- **Verse (source):** "In the case of conception (a) by day, the Sun and Venus represent respectively the father and the mother; and by night, Saturn and the Moon represent respectively the father and the mother. In the former case, Saturn and the Moon represent respectively the paternal uncle and the maternal aunt; and in the latter case the Sun and Venus represent respectively the paternal uncle and the maternal aunt. If the planets representing the father and the paternal uncle should occupy the odd signs, or if the planets representing the mother and the maternal aunt should occupy the even signs at the time, then the father and paternal uncle or the mother and the maternal aunt would be happy."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): "or birth" according to the Commentator — the same scheme may apply to the birth moment.

## R-4.10 — Ascendant or Moon between malefics, not aspected by benefics → pregnant woman dies

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 7
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** At the time, EITHER the rising sign OR the Moon OR both are between malefic planets (Sun, Mars, Saturn — the three malefics, occupying the 12th and 2nd houses from the ascendant or the Moon, or alternate houses; or the Navamsas on either side) AND simultaneously NOT aspected by benefic planets.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** A severely unfavorable timing for undertaking conception — the ascendant or Moon hemmed between malefics without benefic relief is the classical signature of danger to the pregnant woman's life. The constructive path is primary: avoid this window; choose a muhurta where benefics aspect the ascendant and the Moon so the malefic enclosure is broken. The chart reads the moment, not the person — dignified timing choice is the remedy. Where it manifests, it is before delivery, in the month of the powerful malefic.
- **Verse (source):** "If (at the time) either the rising sign or the Moon or both be between malefic planets (a) and at the same time not aspected by the benefic planets, the pregnant woman will die (b)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): malefics here are three — Sun, Mars, Saturn; both ascendant and Moon can be between them when the malefics are together or occupy two alternate houses; the Commentator allows 12th/2nd from ascendant, Moon, or both, or the Navamsas on either side. Footnote (b): before delivery, in the month of the powerful malefic planet.

## R-4.14 — Lagna, Sun, Jupiter, Moon powerful in odd signs & odd Navamsas → male child

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 11
- **Classification:** strength
- **Condition:** At the time of conception (or birth, or query), the rising sign, the Sun, Jupiter, and the Moon are powerful AND occupy odd (male) signs AND odd Navamsas.
- **Effect (one-way, dignity-closing):** Favorable timing for undertaking conception toward a male child: the four-fold male placement — odd signs and odd Navamsas with strength — is the classical signature of a male issue. A clear, gifted window for that intention.
- **Verse (source):** "If (a) the rising sign, the Sun, Jupiter and the Moon be powerful and occupy the odd (male) signs and the odd Navamsas, the issue will be a male child (b); if they occupy the even (female) signs and the even Navamsas, the issue will be a female child (c); if again, the Sun and Jupiter (d) occupy the odd signs, the issue will be a male child, and if the Moon, Venus and Mars (e) occupy the even signs, the issue will be a female child. Also, if the Sun and Jupiter occupy the two male double bodied Navamsas (f) and be aspected by Mercury, the issue will be male twins, and if Venus and Mars occupy the two female double bodied Navamsas (g) and be aspected by Mercury, the issue will be female twins (h)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): at conception, birth, or query. Footnotes (b & c): if some planets are in male and some in female signs/Navamsas, the sex follows whichever predominates. (d & e): the planets should be powerful also. (f): male double-bodied Navamsas = Gemini and Sagittari. (g): female double-bodied Navamsas = Virgo and Pisces. (h): if all four double-bodied Navamsas are so occupied, both male and female twins result. This verse contains four more sub-rules (R-4.15 through R-4.18 below).

## R-4.20 — Six Napumsaka Yogas → hermaphrodite issue

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 13
- **Classification:** neutral
- **Condition:** At the time of conception, ANY ONE of the following six Yogas holds (and the male/female yogas of v 11–12 are absent):
  (1) the Moon (in an even sign) and the Sun (in an odd sign) aspect each other;
  (2) Saturn (in an even sign) and Mercury (in an odd sign) aspect each other;
  (3) Mars (in an odd sign) aspects OR is aspected by the Sun (in an even sign);
  (4) the Moon and Lagna (in odd signs) are aspected by Mars (in an even sign);
  (5) Mars aspects the Moon and Mercury occupying respectively even and odd signs;
  (6) Venus, Lagna, and the Moon occupy male signs and male Navamsas.
