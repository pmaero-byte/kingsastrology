> **Reframing policy (binding for this chapter).** The source text speaks of
> "early death" / balarishta yogas. In this product these are **never** rendered
> as death forecasts for a child. Every balarishta indication is reframed as a
> gentle **"tend carefully in early years"** notice — an invitation to
> protective care, not a prognosis. **Precise death-timing predictions are
> forbidden in this product.** Each `tend` rule below is paired with its
> offsetting strength or constructive framing (Pillar 3: strength before doom),
> and the chapter closes with the commentator's counteracting yogas
> (R-4.13 – R-4.28), which are the primary strengths to surface first in any
> reading that matches a balarishta condition. The death-timing clauses in
> verses 6 and 12 are preserved verbatim in the **Verse (source)** field for

## R-4.1 — Sandhyakala lunar Hora + malefics in last Navamsas; Moon and three malefics in four Kendras

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 1
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** (A) Birth occurs in Sandhyakala (twilight — the period before half-sunrise and after half-sunset, per Brihat Samhita I.30) AND the rising Hora is the lunar Hora (in odd signs the second half, in even signs the first half) AND one or more malefic planets occupy the last Navamsa of their signs; OR (B) the Moon and three malefic planets occupy the four Kendras (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th), one in each.
- **Effect:** These are delicate early-life configurations — tend the child with extra care, warmth, and protective attention in the first years. They are not verdicts: the commentator's counteracting yogas (R-4.13 ff.) — foremost powerful Jupiter in the rising sign — are known to dissolve this indication entirely, and those strengths should be read first.
- **Verse (source):** "If the birth should occur in the Sandhyakala (a) (the twilight hours) when the lunar Hora (b) should be rising and when malefic planets occupy the last Navamsas of signs, the child would die soon after birth. Again, if at the time of birth, the Moon and three malefic planets occupy the four Kendras, one in each, then also the child would die."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): Sandhyakala is defined by the Author in Brihat Samhita, Stanza I, ch. 30: "That period which precedes the moment when the Sun has just half risen and that which succeeds the moment when the Sun has just half set, during which the stars are invisible, known as Sandhyakala or twilight period." Footnote (b): In the odd signs, the second halves, and in the even signs, the first halves are known as Lunar Horas. OCR: "when- the" corrected to "when the". Condition (A) requires a precise Sandhyakala computation — flag for scholar review on the boundary definition.

## R-4.2 — Cancer/Scorpio rising with malefics east and benefics west; malefics in 12th & 2nd or 6th & 8th

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 2
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** (A) The rising sign is Cancer or Scorpio AND malefic planets occupy the eastern half of the Zodiac while benefic planets occupy the western half (the meridian plane divides the Zodiac); OR (B) malefic planets occupy the 12th and the 2nd houses, or the 6th and the 8th houses, from the rising sign.
- **Effect:** These early-life placements call for careful tending in the child's first years; they are invitations to protective care, not forecasts. The arrangement is fully offset when the counteracting yogas (R-4.13 ff.) — especially a powerful lord of the rising sign aspected by benefics in Kendras — are present, and those gifts should be read first.
- **Verse (source):** "If either sign Cancer or sign Scorpio should rise at the time of birth and if malefic planets occupy the eastern half of the Zodiac while the benefic planets occupy the western half (c), the child would die immediately after birth. Again, if malefic planets occupy the 12th and the 2nd houses or the 6th and the 8th houses from the rising sign, the child would die."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (c): the plane of the meridian divides the Zodiac into two equal halves (eastern and western). Footnote (b) on this verse (mislabeled in source): according to certain commentators the two yogas are taken together as a single yoga — i.e. malefics in 2nd, 12th, 6th and 8th. According to Garga, there are four separate yogas: malefics in (1) 6th & 12th, (2) 8th & 2nd, (3) 12th & 2nd, (4) 8th & 6th. OCR corrections: "(0)" → "(c)"; "tbe" → "the". The verse footnote marker in source is "(0)" and "(b)" — see Notes; the (c) corresponds to the eastern/western half note.

## R-4.6 — Moon in 6th or 8th aspected by malefic / benefic / both; benefic in 6th or 8th aspected by powerful malefics; lagna lord in 7th defeated by a malefic

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 6
- **Classification:** tend
- **Condition:** The Moon occupies the 6th or the 8th house from the ascendant. Sub-cases: (A) a malefic planet aspects the Moon; (B) only benefic planets aspect it; (C) both a benefic and a malefic aspect it; (D) a benefic planet occupying the 6th or 8th is aspected by powerful malefic planets; (E) the lord of the rising sign occupies the 7th house and is defeated in planetary fight (conjunction) by a malefic planet. Per the verse note, if the Moon in 6th/8th is not aspected by any planet, there is no early-life indication.
- **Effect:** This is a delicate early-life configuration to tend with care and protection; it is not a death forecast. The verse itself names the offsetting strengths: if the 6th/8th occupied by the Moon is the house of a benefic, or the Moon is accompanied by a benefic, the child is protected; and if the Moon in 6th/8th is not aspected by any planet, there is no early-life danger. Per Mandavya, birth by day in the dark half, or by night in the bright half, also gives escape. The counteracting yogas (R-4.13 ff.) apply as well. **Effect intentionally omits the verse's death-age (8 years / 4 years) and death-month clauses; scholar review required before any time-bound language is reintroduced.**
- **Verse (source):** "If, when the Moon occupies the 6th or the 8th house from the ascendant, a malefic planet aspect the same, the child will die soon after birth: if benefic planets alone aspect it, the child will die after 8 years; and if both a benefic and a malefic planet aspect the Moon, the child will die 4 years after birth (a); but if a benefic planet (occupying the 6th or the 8th house) be aspected by powerful malefic planets (b), or if the lord of the rising sign occupy the 7th house and be defeated in planetary fight (conjunction) by a malefic planet (c), the child will die n month after birth."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): if the Moon in 6th/8th is not aspected by any planet, no early death. Yavaneswara: if the 6th/8th occupied by the Moon is a benefic's house, or the Moon is accompanied by a benefic (even in a malefic sign), the child will not die. Mandavya: birth by day in the dark half, or by night in the bright half, escapes even if the Moon in 6th/8th is aspected by both malefic and benefic. Footnote (b): in the absence of benefics aspecting the Moon, adds the Commentator; Swalpa Jataka has: "By malefic planets or by planets retrograde in their motion." Footnote (c): the planet that generally suffers defeat in conjunction is the southern one, one of forbidding appearance, one of flickering light, one retrograde, one of small size, or in conjunction with the Sun, or of unusual appearance or without light or color. OCR: "n month" is in the source (likely "in a month"); "aspect tbe" → "aspect the"; "1"OTFS" → "NOTES". Per the reframing policy, the time-bound clauses are preserved verbatim above but excluded from the Effect.

## R-4.12 — Timing of the indicated early-life crisis (Moon reaching the powerful death-causing planet's sign, her own birth sign, or the rising sign, powerful and aspected by powerful malefics; period a year)

- **Cites:** Ch 4, v 12

- **Condition:** A balarishta indication (R-4.1 – R-4.11) is matched AND the Moon, in transit, reaches either (A) the sign occupied by the powerful planet causing the indication, or (B) the sign occupied by the Moon at birth, or (C) the rising sign at birth; AND the Moon is both powerful and aspected by powerful malefic planets. The Munis say the period for such an event is a year.
- **Effect:** This verse concerns the timing of an early-life sensitivity window; **per the reframing policy, the product renders no death-timing prediction.** The constructive reading is that the early years call for watchful, protective care, especially around the Moon's transit returns noted above — and that the counteracting yogas (R-4.13 ff.) and benefic support are the strengths to emphasize. Scholar review is required on whether any time-window language may be used.
- **Verse (source):** "The death will occur when the Moon comes either to the sign occupied by the powerful planet causing such death, or to the sign occupied by herself at the time of birth or to the rising sign at the time, provided the Moon is both powerful and aspected by powerful malefic planets; and the Munis say that the period for such death is a year (a)."
- **Status:** draft
- **Notes:** Footnote (a): the Moon makes about 13 revolutions in the course of a year; whenever the Moon in motion becomes powerful and is aspected by powerful malefic planets on reaching one of the three places mentioned, the event occurs.
