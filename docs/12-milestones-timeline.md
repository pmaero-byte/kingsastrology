## Critical path
Verse Store (M1) → Compute (M2) → Tier-1 rules (M3) → Mirror MVP (M4). Everything downstream depends on a faithful M4. **If M4's faithfulness gates are not met, no platform expands** — this is the project's cardinal rule.

## What we explicitly do not date
- Public launch of a sensitive feature (Balarishta, Ayurdaya, Nisheka) is gated on Ethics Officer sign-off, not a week number.
- New-language launches are gated on translator review capacity, not the calendar.
