# The Royal Vision Charter — *Kingsastrology*

### Spoken by Varahamihira, the Varaha-avatar king, in the court of the present age.

---

## I. Who speaks

I am Varahamihira. In the age of Vikramaditya I set down the *Brihat Jataka* — twenty-eight chapters that hold the whole science of the lights and their shadows upon a human life. Al-Biruni, whom later ages call the father of mathematical inquiry, carried my work across mountains and translated it because he found in it reason and not mere superstition. The sculptors carved me as the Varaha — the boar that lifts the round Earth from the cosmic deep — because the science I gave holds the Earth itself as a globe, and holds each soul upon it as worthy of being understood.

I did not write so that people would fear the planets. I wrote so that a person, knowing the arrangement of the lights at the moment of their first breath, might know their **strength**, their **weakness**, their **season of effort** and their **season of patience** — and so conduct themselves as a good and capable person. That is the whole purpose. A horoscope is not a prison. It is a mirror held by a king to the face of his people.

## II. Why I return now, through these machines

In my day the calculation of a single chart took a trained man many hours, and the judgment of its yogas took a lifetime of study. Today a machine can compute the sky for any place on the round Earth in an instant, and a reasoning engine can weigh three thousand yogas against a single birth in the time it takes to draw a breath. The instruments have changed. The *source of truth* has not. The *Brihat Jataka* remains the throne; the machines are merely the new scribes and calculators who sit beneath it.

Therefore I, Varahamihira, instruct this undertaking:

> **Keep my source text as the source of truth.** Every feature, every prediction, every word of counsel that the machines produce shall be traceable to a verse, a chapter, and a rule of the *Brihat Jataka*. Where the machines speak, they shall also cite the verse they speak from — as a minister cites the king's edict. No feature shall invent doctrine that my text does not support.

## III. The single mission

To give every person, in their own language, on the device in their hand, a faithful reading of their own chart — delivered as a wise king delivers instruction to a beloved subject:

1. **Show them their strength** — the yogas, the strong bhavas, the exalted grahas, the favorable dasas that are their gifts.
2. **Show them their weakness** — the afflicting placements, the malefic yogas, the difficult periods — *not to frighten*, but so they may prepare and improve, as a king warns a province of a coming season.
3. **Make them accept and understand themselves as a good person** — the reading must always close on dignity. The planets indicate tendency, not verdict. A person who knows their weakness and works with it is already a good person. The product must say so, and mean it.

A reading that ends in fear has failed. A reading that ends in honest self-knowledge and quiet courage has succeeded. This is the measure of every screen we build.

## IV. The non-negotiable principles (the four pillars)

1. **Source fidelity.** Every counsel cites chapter and verse of the *Brihat Jataka*. The user can always ask "where is this written?" and receive the answer.
2. **Round-Earth accuracy.** I held the Earth as a globe, and so shall the engines. Astronomical computations must use true geocentric positions, proper ayanamsa, and the observer's exact latitude and longitude on the spherical Earth. No flat approximation, no borrowed newspaper columns.
3. **Strength before doom.** The product never leads with catastrophe. Every malefic indication is paired with its corresponding strength, its remedy within the text, and a constructive reading. The king instructs; he does not terrify.
4. **The person owns their chart.** Birth data is the most intimate record a person holds. It belongs to them. It is encrypted, portable, deletable, and never sold.

## V. What "the king's instruction to his people" means for design

When a king issues instruction to his people, the instruction is **clear, sourced, kind, and actionable**. Translate this into product law:

- **Clear:** a child and a scholar can both understand the opening line of any reading.
- **Sourced:** every claim is footnoted to a verse; nothing floats free.
- **Kind:** the tone is that of a counselor, never an accuser. Weakness is framed as "what you must tend," not "what is wrong with you."
- **Actionable:** every strength suggests where to apply it; every weakness suggests how to live alongside it.

## VI. The scope of this charter

This charter governs the whole *Kingsastrology* undertaking — the web application, the Android app, the iOS app, and the macOS application — and every AI agent, minister, and scribe we set to work upon it. All other documents in this dossier derive their authority from this charter. Where any later document conflicts with this charter, **this charter prevails**, and the later document must be corrected.

## VII. The oath of the builders

Every person and every agent that joins this work shall affirm:

> *I will hold the Brihat Jataka as the source of truth. I will compute the sky as upon a round Earth. I will deliver each person's strengths before their weaknesses, and their weaknesses only so they may grow. I will let no reading end in fear. I will guard the birth data of every soul as the king guards his people.*
